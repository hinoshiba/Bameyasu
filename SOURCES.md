## Japan: office lighting

- Source: e-Gov, `事務所衛生基準規則` (Ordinance on Health Standards in the Office), Article 10
- Official URL: https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/347M50002000043
- Fact used: ordinary office work surfaces require at least 300 lx; marked contrast and glare should be avoided.
- Applicability: Japanese office employers. Bameyasu uses the value for education, not compliance determination.
- Rights note: Japanese legislation is excluded from copyright protection under Copyright Act Article 13. Site identity and URL are attributed.

## Japan: information-equipment work

- Source: Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, `情報機器作業における労働衛生管理のためのガイドライン`
- Official URL: https://www.mhlw.go.jp/web/t_doc?dataId=00tc6314&dataType=1
- Facts used: about 40 cm or more viewing distance; screen top at or below eye level; glare reduction; work-surface lighting; sessions no longer than one hour; short pauses and 10–15 minute work breaks.
- Processing notice: summarized and organized by Bameyasu. No ministry logo or source illustration is used.
- Terms: https://www.mhlw.go.jp/chosakuken/

## Occupational noise

- Source: U.S. CDC / National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health, occupational noise guidance
- Official URL: https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/noise/about/noise.html
- Criteria document: https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/docs/98-126/pdfs/98-126.pdf
- Fact used: the NIOSH REL is 85 dBA as an 8-hour time-weighted average, with a 3 dB exchange rate.
- Important scope: the value concerns occupational hearing exposure, not an optimal focus-room target. Bameyasu's short phone measurement cannot establish the 8-hour TWA.
- Rights/endorsement: many U.S. government works are public domain, but third-party elements may not be. Bameyasu provides attribution and does not imply CDC/NIOSH endorsement. https://www.cdc.gov/other/agencymaterials.html

## Monitor distance and posture

- Source: U.S. Occupational Safety and Health Administration, Computer Workstations eTool — Monitors
- Official URL: https://www.osha.gov/etools/computer-workstations/components/monitors
- Fact used: generally preferred viewing distance is 20–40 inches (50–100 cm), while readability and upright posture take priority.
- Bameyasu uses the Japanese MHLW “about 40 cm or more” as its primary checkbox and presents OSHA's range as additional context.

## Japan: building environmental health

- Source: Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, `建築物環境衛生管理基準`
- Official URL: https://www.mhlw.go.jp/bunya/kenkou/seikatsu-eisei10/index.html
- Facts used: for covered air-conditioned rooms, temperature 18–28°C, relative humidity 40–70%, CO₂ at or below 1000 ppm, and CO at or below 6 ppm.
- Important scope: these criteria do not mean that 1000 ppm CO₂ is an acute-toxicity boundary, and they do not replace individual comfort assessment or appropriate professional measurement. Bameyasu cannot measure them without an external instrument.
- Processing notice: summarized and organized by Bameyasu under the ministry's content terms; no source logo or figure is used.
