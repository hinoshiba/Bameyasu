# Community Code of Conduct

Bameyasu welcomes respectful participation regardless of background, identity, experience, ability, or viewpoint.

Be constructive, discuss the work rather than the person, cite evidence for health and measurement claims, respect privacy, and accept good-faith correction. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate misinformation, and publishing another person's sensor or health-related data are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security/contact channel. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially and fairly as practical.
