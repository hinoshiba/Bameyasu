Measure your workspace.

Bameyasu is an on-device SwiftUI app that uses your iPhone to review light, sound, desk vibration, and ergonomics, then suggests one practical action to try first.

01 · 10 sec
☀

Lighting

Bameyasu estimates work-surface illuminance and contrast from rear wide-camera ISO, exposure duration, aperture, and relative luminance across nine frame regions.

300 lx is a reference for ordinary office work, not a compliance measurement.
02 · 15 sec

Sound

Bameyasu prefers 48 kHz input and applies A-weighting when the actual input is near 48 kHz, then estimates an equivalent level on device. It performs no speech recognition and creates no audio file.

Before comparison calibration it is shown as “ref. dB*” and excluded from readiness.
03 · 8 sec
◎

Desk stability

Bameyasu uses Core Motion at 30 Hz to review acceleration RMS and tilt against its own guide, helping you notice a wobbly desk or vibrating equipment.

This is not a health, safety, or structural assessment and is excluded from readiness.
04 · Self-check
⌁

Ergonomics

Seven items cover screen distance and height, shoulders and elbows, back and lower back, feet, glare, and breaks in continuous work.

An unchecked item means “not confirmed,” not necessarily “bad.”

How it works

About 60 seconds,
three steps.

You can skip a measurement if you decline its permission and continue the check. Permissions are requested only when needed.

  1. 1

    Start at your usual desk

    Place your iPhone where you normally work, with your setup in its usual state.

  2. 2

    Follow the guidance

    Review light, sound, desk stability, and ergonomics in order.

  3. 3

    Improve one thing first

    Try the highest-priority suggestion, then compare it with your next saved check.

Measurement approach

Never fill gaps
with guesses.

Workspace readiness v0.1.0 is Bameyasu's own guide for prioritizing improvements. It is not a public index or a health or safety score.

60%Estimated light
40%Ergonomics

Comparison-calibrated sound only: +35%
Available weights are normalized to 100. Readiness is not shown unless both light and ergonomics are available.

Included

Confidence stays explicit

Estimated light is weighted 60% and ergonomics 40%. Sound is added only after the user confirms comparison calibration against a reference meter. Unavailable values are omitted.

Excluded

Values with a different meaning

Desk vibration, uncalibrated sound, and unavailable values are excluded from readiness. A 15-second sound reading is not an eight-hour time-weighted average, and comparison calibration still does not make Bameyasu a statutory meter.

Not guaranteed

Do not substitute it for judgment

A high readiness score means only that the items Bameyasu could check were within its current guide. It does not guarantee health, safety, comfort, productivity, or legal compliance.

Not measured by iPhone alone

Appropriate external instruments are required.

Privacy

Your workspace data
stays in your workspace.

Bameyasu does not collect data from the app. It has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or cloud synchronization.

Effective: August 16, 2026

ONDEVICE

Sensor input is processed in place

Camera, microphone, and motion input are processed live on device only during the corresponding check. Images, video, audio, and raw sensor data are not saved or transmitted, and Bameyasu performs no speech recognition.

01

Data stored on the device

Completed-check times, calculated metrics, checklist answers, recommendations, scoring-algorithm versions, and settings are stored in the app sandbox.

02

Data not stored

History does not contain images, audio, raw frames or samples, contacts, location, identifiers, or advertising data.

03

Deletion and permissions

You can delete history entries individually. Deleting the app removes its sandboxed data, and permissions can be revoked at any time in iPhone Settings.

04

External links

The app does not contact a server by itself. If you open an external link, the destination receives ordinary web-request information such as your IP address and user agent under its own privacy policy.

05

Disclosure and changes

For data collected automatically by the app, the intended App Store privacy response is “Data Not Collected.” Voluntary support email will also be considered during every release and feature review.

06

Children's information

Bameyasu is a general productivity tool and does not knowingly collect personal information through the app from anyone, including children.

07

Support communications

If you email support@hinoshiba.com, the sender address, headers, message, and optional attachments are received through the email provider. They are kept only as long as reasonably needed to reply, provide support, investigate reports, prevent abuse, or meet legal and security obligations. You may request deletion at the same address, subject to those obligations.

Terms and measurement disclaimer

Use it safely,
as reference information.

Bameyasu provides reference information for reviewing a workspace. Please use it with the following conditions in mind.

Effective: August 15, 2026

  1. It is not a professional instrument or medical service

    Bameyasu is not a medical device, diagnostic service, treatment, disease-prevention service, calibrated lux meter, calibrated sound-level meter, or statutory workplace measurement instrument.

  2. Results are not guarantees

    Results include estimates and Bameyasu-specific heuristics. They do not guarantee health, safety, comfort, productivity, or legal compliance.

  3. Values vary with the environment and device

    Camera and microphone estimates vary by iPhone model, case, microphone path, placement, direction, reflections, light spectrum, wind, handling, ambient sound, and calibration.

  4. Do not use it as the sole basis for important decisions

    Do not use Bameyasu as the sole basis for health, employment, occupational-safety, emergency, or regulatory decisions.

  5. When danger is suspected, put safety before the display

    If symptoms, severe discomfort, hazardous sound, combustion gas, smoke, heat, or another danger is suspected, leave the area regardless of Bameyasu's display and contact an employer, occupational physician, medical provider, emergency service, or qualified measurement professional as appropriate.

85 dBA as an eight-hour time-weighted average with a 3 dB exchange rate. This is not a focus-room target, and a 15-second reading cannot establish eight-hour exposure.

A generally preferred viewing distance of 50–100 cm, used as additional context while prioritizing readability and upright posture.
