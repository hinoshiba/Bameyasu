Four ways to put your iPhone to work

Light, sound, motion, and posture
in one guided flow.

The camera, microphone, motion sensors, and self-checks work together to visualize your workspace from several angles. Sensor input is processed on device, and history keeps only the information needed for results.

01 · 10 sec

Lighting

Bameyasu estimates work-surface illuminance and contrast from rear wide-camera ISO, exposure duration, aperture, and relative luminance across nine frame regions.

Use the 300 lx reference for ordinary office work as a starting point when reviewing your lighting—not as a compliance determination.
02 · 15 sec

Sound

Visualize a short-term reference for surrounding sound in 15 seconds. When the device supplies input near 48 kHz, Bameyasu applies A-weighting and calculates a reference value on device. It does not recognize speech or create an audio file.

Before comparison calibration, “ref. dB*” gives you a cue for reviewing quietness; after calibration, sound can also contribute to readiness.
03 · 8 sec

Desk stability

Bameyasu uses Core Motion at 30 Hz to visualize small desk movements and tilt, giving you a practical cue for finding wobble or vibrating equipment.

It stays separate from readiness as a practical desk-adjustment reference, not a health, safety, or structural assessment.
04 · Self-check

Ergonomics

Seven items cover screen distance and height, shoulders and elbows, back and lower back, feet, glare, and breaks in continuous work.

Confirmed items contribute to the result, and the first unchecked item can become the next point to review.

How it works

About 60 seconds,
three steps.

Choose the checks you want to run. Each permission is requested only when its step begins, and you can continue when a check is skipped.

  1. 1

    Start at your usual desk

    Place your iPhone where you normally work, with your setup in its usual state.

  2. 2

    Follow the guidance

    Review light, sound, desk stability, and ergonomics in order.

  3. 3

    Improve one thing first

    Try the highest-priority suggestion, then compare it with your next saved check.

Readiness inputs

Combine each signal at the right confidence

Estimated light contributes 60% and ergonomics 40%. Sound joins after the user confirms comparison calibration against a reference meter, building readiness from the available inputs.

Separate insights

Use desk and sound as practical cues

Desk vibration and uncalibrated sound remain visible on their own, separate from readiness. Desk vibration is not a health, safety, or structural assessment. A 15-second sound level is a short-term reference, not a substitute for an eight-hour time-weighted average or statutory measurement.

Review the change

Compare before and after consistently

Workspace readiness is Bameyasu's own summary of the items checked. Repeat the same flow at the same desk to review progress and choose the next step. It is not used to guarantee or determine health, safety, comfort, productivity, or legal compliance.

Privacy

Checks and history
stay on your iPhone.

Bameyasu works without accounts, advertising, cloud synchronization, or analytics and crash-reporting SDKs. Checks run on device, and history and settings stay on your iPhone.

Effective: August 16, 2026

ONDEVICE

Sensor input is processed on your iPhone

Camera, microphone, and motion inputs are processed live on device only during the corresponding check, then discarded without being saved or transmitted. Bameyasu does not perform speech recognition. History stores completed-check summaries, while settings are kept separately on device.

01

Data stored on the device

Completed-check times, calculated metrics, checklist answers, recommendations, scoring-algorithm versions, and settings are stored in the app sandbox.

02

History keeps only what results need

History stores only the information needed for check results and leaves out images, audio, raw frames or samples, contacts, location, identifiers, and advertising data.

03

Deletion and permissions

You can delete history entries individually. Deleting the app removes its sandboxed data, and permissions can be revoked at any time in iPhone Settings.

04

External links open only when you choose

Checks run without contacting a Bameyasu server. When you choose an external link, its destination receives ordinary web-request information such as your IP address and user agent under its own privacy policy.

05

Disclosure reviewed with every release

The intended App Store response for data collected automatically by the app is “Data Not Collected.” Every release and feature review also accounts for any support email a user chooses to send.

06

One data policy for every user

As a general productivity tool, Bameyasu applies the same data policy to every user and does not knowingly collect personal information through the app from anyone, including children.

07

Support messages stay in your control

When you choose to email support@hinoshiba.com, the maintainer receives your sender address, headers, message, and optional attachments through the hinoshiba.com email service provider. That information is used only to reply, provide support, investigate reports, prevent abuse, or meet legal and security obligations, and retained only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes or obligations. You may request deletion at the same address, subject to those obligations.

Terms and measurement disclaimer

Important context
for confident use.

These points explain the role of each result and the safety guidance that helps you use Bameyasu as a practical improvement tool.

Effective: August 15, 2026

  1. Purpose and role

    Bameyasu is a wellness, education, and productivity tool. It is not a medical device, diagnostic service, treatment, disease-prevention service, calibrated lux meter, calibrated sound-level meter, or statutory workplace measurement instrument.

  2. How to read each result

    Use the estimates and Bameyasu-specific heuristics to prioritize practical improvements. They do not guarantee health, safety, comfort, productivity, or legal compliance.

  3. Values vary with the environment and device

    Camera and microphone estimates vary by iPhone model, case, microphone path, placement, direction, reflections, light spectrum, wind, handling, ambient sound, and calibration.

  4. Decisions that need qualified guidance

    For health, employment, occupational-safety, emergency, or regulatory decisions, do not rely on Bameyasu alone; use the appropriate qualified guidance or formal measurement.

  5. Put safety first

    If symptoms, severe discomfort, hazardous sound, combustion gas, smoke, heat, or another danger is suspected, leave the area regardless of Bameyasu's display and contact an employer, occupational physician, medical provider, emergency service, or qualified measurement professional as appropriate.

  6. Relationship to public sources

    Each card links directly to the source context. Bameyasu independently organizes the key points; the publishers do not endorse or approve the app.

e-Gov Ordinance on Health Standards in the Office, Article 10

Open official source ↗ Japan · Information-equipment work

Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare guidelines

Covers a viewing distance of about 40 cm or more, screen-top position, glare reduction, sessions no longer than one hour, and 10–15 minute work breaks.

Open official source ↗ U.S. · Occupational noise

CDC / NIOSH

For occupational-exposure context, NIOSH uses 85 dBA as an eight-hour time-weighted average with a 3 dB exchange rate. Bameyasu keeps its 15-second value separate as a short-term cue; it cannot establish eight-hour exposure.

Open official source ↗ U.S. · Monitor setup

OSHA Computer Workstations

A generally preferred viewing distance of 50–100 cm, used as additional context while prioritizing readability and upright posture.

For covered air-conditioned rooms: 18–28°C, 40–70% relative humidity, CO₂ at or below 1,000 ppm, and CO at or below 6 ppm. These conditions require appropriate external instruments; Bameyasu does not measure them or determine compliance.
