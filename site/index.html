iPhoneで約60秒

仕事場を見える化して、
次の一歩を選びやすく。

Bameyasuは、iPhoneのカメラ・マイク・モーションセンサーとセルフチェックで、光、音、机の揺れ、姿勢と配置を約60秒で見える化。結果を「環境の整い度」と「まず直す1つ」にまとめ、整える前後の変化を振り返れます。

アプリが案内する順序と、結果の使い方が分かる5場面です。表示値と履歴は説明用のサンプルで、実際の結果は端末や環境によって変わります。

iPhoneを活かす4つの視点

光・音・動き・姿勢を、
ひとつの流れで。

カメラ、マイク、モーションセンサーとセルフチェックを組み合わせ、仕事場の状態を多角的に見える化します。センサー入力は端末内で処理し、履歴には結果に必要な情報だけを保存します。

01 · 10秒

光環境

背面ワイドカメラのISO・露出時間・絞り値と、画面内9領域の相対輝度から作業面の照度と明暗差を推定します。

一般的な事務作業の300 lxを参考に、明るさを見直すきっかけを示します。法令適合を判定する測定ではありません。
02 · 15秒

音環境

周囲の音を15秒の参考値として可視化します。端末が48 kHz付近の入力を提供する場合はA特性処理を適用し、端末内で参考値を算出します。音声内容は認識せず、音声ファイルも作りません。

比較校正前は「参考 dB*」として静けさを見直す手がかりを表示し、比較校正後は整い度にも反映します。
03 · 8秒

机の安定性

Core Motionを30 Hzで使い、机の小さな揺れと傾きを可視化します。がたつきや振動源を見つける手がかりに使えます。

整い度とは分けて、机まわりを整えるための参考値として表示します。健康・安全・構造評価には使用できません。
04 · 自己確認

姿勢と配置

画面距離と高さ、肩・肘、背中・腰、足裏、グレア、作業の区切りを7項目で確認します。

確認済みの項目数を結果に反映し、最初の未確認項目を次に見直すポイントとして提案できます。

別枠で活用

机と音も改善の手がかりに

机の揺れと未校正の音は個別に表示し、整い度と分けて改善に活用できます。机の値は健康・安全・構造評価ではありません。15秒の音量は短時間の参考値で、8時間の時間加重平均や法定測定の代わりにはなりません。

変化の振り返り

同じ手順で前後を比べる

環境の整い度は、確認できた項目をまとめたBameyasu独自の目安です。同じ席・手順で記録し、改善前後の変化と次の一歩を考えるために使えます。健康、安全、快適性、生産性、法令適合の保証や判定には使用できません。

専用機器で別途確認

空気・温熱などの環境は、用途に合った専用機器や専門的な方法で確認します。

Bameyasuはこれらを測定対象にせず、必要に応じて適切な外部測定器で確認する項目として案内します。

About 60 seconds on iPhone

See your workspace clearly.
Choose the next step.

Bameyasu uses the iPhone camera, microphone, motion sensors, and self-checks to visualize light, sound, desk vibration, and ergonomics in about 60 seconds. It summarizes the check with workspace readiness and “Fix this first,” then lets you review before-and-after changes.

Four ways to put your iPhone to work

Light, sound, motion, and posture
in one guided flow.

The camera, microphone, motion sensors, and self-checks work together to visualize your workspace from several angles. Sensor input is processed on device, and history keeps only the information needed for results.

01 · 10 sec

Lighting

Bameyasu estimates work-surface illuminance and contrast from rear wide-camera ISO, exposure duration, aperture, and relative luminance across nine frame regions.

Use the 300 lx reference for ordinary office work as a starting point when reviewing your lighting—not as a compliance determination.
02 · 15 sec

Sound

Visualize a short-term reference for surrounding sound in 15 seconds. When the device supplies input near 48 kHz, Bameyasu applies A-weighting and calculates a reference value on device. It does not recognize speech or create an audio file.

Before comparison calibration, “ref. dB*” gives you a cue for reviewing quietness; after calibration, sound can also contribute to readiness.
03 · 8 sec

Desk stability

Bameyasu uses Core Motion at 30 Hz to visualize small desk movements and tilt, giving you a practical cue for finding wobble or vibrating equipment.

It stays separate from readiness as a practical desk-adjustment reference, not a health, safety, or structural assessment.
04 · Self-check

Ergonomics

Seven items cover screen distance and height, shoulders and elbows, back and lower back, feet, glare, and breaks in continuous work.

Confirmed items contribute to the result, and the first unchecked item can become the next point to review.

How it works

About 60 seconds,
three steps.

Choose the checks you want to run. Each permission is requested only when its step begins, and you can continue when a check is skipped.

  1. 1

    Start at your usual desk

    Place your iPhone where you normally work, with your setup in its usual state.

  2. 2

    Follow the guidance

    Review light, sound, desk stability, and ergonomics in order.

  3. 3

    Improve one thing first

    Try the highest-priority suggestion, then compare it with your next saved check.

Compare before and after

Put your iPhone's built-in sensors to work
and make before-and-after changes visible.

Run camera, microphone, motion-sensor, and self-check steps in one consistent flow, then save the calculated results. Light and ergonomics form the core, comparison-calibrated sound can join them, and repeated checks help you review what changed after an improvement.

Readiness inputs

Combine each signal at the right confidence

Estimated light contributes 60% and ergonomics 40%. Sound joins after the user confirms comparison calibration against a reference meter, building readiness from the available inputs.

Separate insights

Use desk and sound as practical cues

Desk vibration and uncalibrated sound remain visible on their own, separate from readiness. Desk vibration is not a health, safety, or structural assessment. A 15-second sound level is a short-term reference, not a substitute for an eight-hour time-weighted average or statutory measurement.

Review the change

Compare before and after consistently

Workspace readiness is Bameyasu's own summary of the items checked. Repeat the same flow at the same desk to review progress and choose the next step. It is not used to guarantee or determine health, safety, comfort, productivity, or legal compliance.

Use the right external instrument or professional method for each condition.

Privacy policy

Privacy

Updated September 6, 2026

Data use

Camera, microphone, and motion input are processed on the iPhone during a check. Images, video, audio, and raw sensor data are not saved or sent to the developer. Check results and settings are stored on the device.

Deletion and permissions

Delete history from the History screen and remove the app to delete its local settings. Camera, microphone, and motion permissions can be changed in iPhone Settings.
